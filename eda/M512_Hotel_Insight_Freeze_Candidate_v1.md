# M512 HOTEL — INSIGHT FREEZE CANDIDATE v1

Status: CANDIDATE, NOT FINAL

## Eligible pearls
1. Scale: 37.04% of 119,390 bookings were cancelled.
2. Timing: cancellation rises from about 9.6% at 0–7 days lead time to about 67.7% at 366+ days; direction persists by hotel and year and remains in the adjusted robustness model.
3. Concentration: Online TA, Groups and Offline TA/TO account for about 93% of cancellations.
4. Hotel context: Groups are about 68.9% cancelled at City Hotel vs 42.4% at Resort Hotel; Offline TA/TO about 42.8% vs 15.2%.
5. Recorded prior successful-booking history: ~5.5% cancellation vs ~38.0% when none is recorded. CAVEAT: source sets previous non-cancelled bookings to 0 when no customer profile is associated.
6. Action: target investigation/policy testing at concentrated exposure; do not infer causation from observational associations.
